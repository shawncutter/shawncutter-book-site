# LexDocs/essays

Long-form essays on AI and energy, in Shawn's voice, that also feed the P2L book. Every piece here carries `kind: essay`, which is what keeps essays out of the book: `compile-manuscript.js` compiles `kind: scene` only, so an essay can never become a chapter by accident. They are still indexed and searchable in the dashboard, because they are live writing material — `kind` decides what a document *is*, and `status` decides how finished it is. See `LexDocs/_meta/_schema.md`.

Rules: every factual line traces to Shawn's own words; Claude final-edits before anything is clear-ready; NotebookLM explainer on shawncutter@gmail.com after; nothing posts without Shawn's clear. EnergiAcres and Terrasow are never combined. No MW/GW, scale, or well-count claims in public without his clear. Allusari stays off the page.
