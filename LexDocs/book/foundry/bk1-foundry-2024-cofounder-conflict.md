## Scene Draft

Early on, we had two co-founders. Then several of them teamed up and tried to take over the company.

Everyone was remote. We're all dialing into meetings. They all come at me, saying they wanted a 25% split each way, rather than the original agreed amounts.

One guy tried to become the president and CEO. Out of nowhere.

I shut it down. He was gone the next day.

Only one remained: Augustine. The only one that had true heart in it.

What I didn't know until later was that Augustine had talked to that guy. Told him absolutely not. He wasn't going to be part of it. That surfaced later, when we talked about it.

We went to Burning Man in 2025. This all went down in 2024.

We stayed together. We fought it out, pivoted, found a new path. Built a new team. Added Timur as CFO. Added Dilshad as chief legal.

The battles with the CFO: working with people that haven't built a company from the ground up. People who don't realize all the different things you have to push, not just when it's your job.

Augustine and I took on more. Then I corrected myself: actually just me.

"I just said, I'm going to do it all. I don't care."

The work was maps. People want to do maps. They wouldn't set up. They wouldn't put the parcels together. They wouldn't put the information together.

"I'll use AI and I'll fuckin' do it all."

Pushback: AI slop. The person who gives the most grief for AI slop is chief legal Dilshad. He checks everything like it's a final version because he's a lawyer. I told him to treat it as not final. "But sometimes it should be inferred."

The current team: Dilshad. Augustine. Timur. Pearson, recently added. And me.

Five people. Including me.
