# Questions for Carina — For the Book

*Carina — Shawn's writing the book, but parts of it belong to you, and he can't write them — only you can. This isn't an interview. Hit record on your phone and just talk, or write it out. Ramble, skip around, take your time. The unpolished version is the one we want. Nothing here has to end up in the book unless you're okay with it. There are no wrong answers.*

*The thread running through all of it: **at each stage, what did you see that he couldn't — and what did you wish you could have said to him then?***

---

## Part 1 — Who you are (so you're on the page, not just in the story)

- Where did you grow up, and what was your family like? What did you carry into adulthood from it?
- What do you do professionally — and what's the work actually been across the years?
- How would you describe yourself to someone who's never met you — the parts Shawn might not think to mention?
- What do you love that has nothing to do with Shawn or the kids?

## Part 2 — The beginning

- Miami University, the "scooter guy" with the handicap placard you first thought was a jerk — what's your version of how you met and fell for him?
- Vegas, 2006, the Elvis package, the dress breaking, calling your parents from the limo who hated it — walk through that day from your side.
- When you married him, what did you think you were signing up for? What did you actually get?
- What did you wish you could have said to him in those earliest years — and didn't?

## Part 3 — Building Fielding (the garage years)

- You became a lease agent and that money paid for Fielding. What was it like going to work every day so he could chase a company? Did it feel unfair, or did it feel like *yours* too?
- Take me into that grind — the long hours, the uncertainty, being married to someone always three steps ahead of his own feet.
- What did you carry in those years that he never saw?
- Was there a moment you were scared it wouldn't work? A moment you knew it would?

## Part 4 — The good stretch (Quorum)

- This was probably the best run for the two of you. What made it good? What did the money and stability actually change between you?
- What do you remember from that period that he might've been too busy to notice?
- Anything you wish you'd told him about how good it was — or a warning about what came next?

## Part 5 — The hard turn, and now

- **Feb 2022 — the hardest, most painful time.** Only say what you want to say. What was it like from inside it?
- **The emotional wall** — when he couldn't feel the big moments, even holding Gia. What was it like to love someone behind that wall? When did you first notice it?
- Therapy since 2023, "incrementally better week by week" — what's actually better now that you can point to?
- **The big one:** all the things you wished you could have said to him across these years — say them now. To him. On the record.

## Part 6 — Family

- What's your relationship with Beth like?
- What do you want Gia and Tessa to understand someday about these years?
- How do you feel about Shawn writing all of this down — including the parts that involve you?
