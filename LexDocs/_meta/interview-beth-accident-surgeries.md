# For Mom (Beth) — The Accident & the Surgery Years

*Her story, in her words. This is the single biggest hole in the book: everything about the accident and the 40 surgeries is told from Shawn's side. Beth lived it as the mother. Hand her this, let her talk (record it if she'll allow), and don't rush her.*

**How to use it:** Read one question, then be quiet. Long pauses are where the real material lives. She can skip anything. There are no wrong answers — we want feeling and detail, not a report.

---

## 1. The day of the accident
- Where were you and what were you doing when you found out?
- Who told you, and what exactly did they say?
- What is the first image or feeling you remember from that moment?
- What was the drive/rush to him like? What went through your head?
- When you first saw him — the injuries, the hospital — what do you remember?

## 2. The first hours and days
- Wooster Community Hospital, then the life-flight to Akron Children's — what was that stretch like for you?
- What were the doctors telling you? What were you most afraid of?
- Did you let yourself cry, or did you hold it together for everyone else? Why?
- Where were CJ and the other kids? Who was holding the family together at home?

## 3. The surgery years (there were ~40)
- How did you get through that many surgeries? What did a "surgery day" look like for you?
- Which surgery was the hardest on you — and why that one?
- Was there a moment you thought you might lose him? What happened?
- How did you talk to Shawn about it? Did you protect him from how scared you were?
- What did it cost you — physically, emotionally, financially — that nobody saw?

## 4. Marriage, family, and carrying it
- What was your relationship with Chuck like through those years? Did the accident pull you together or apart?
- Did you feel alone in it? Who, if anyone, actually helped you?
- How did it change the other kids? Did any of them get lost in the shuffle while Shawn was in and out of hospitals?

## 5. Looking back
- What are you proudest of in how you handled it?
- What do you wish you'd done differently?
- Is there something you've never told Shawn about that time that you'd want him to know now?
- If you could say one thing to the version of yourself standing in that hospital hallway — what would it be?
