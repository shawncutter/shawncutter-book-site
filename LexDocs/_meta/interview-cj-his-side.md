# For CJ — Your Side of It

*The book has CJ almost entirely through Shawn's eyes. If CJ answers even a few of these honestly, it's some of the most powerful material in the whole memoir. Shawn's working theory: CJ carries unspoken guilt over the accident — he sent Shawn on the trip CJ himself was supposed to be on. These questions give CJ room to speak for himself without accusation.*

**How to use it:** Low pressure. Frame it as "help me get the story right — I only have my half." Let him answer in writing or on a call. Any answer is better than none.

---

## 1. Growing up
- What do you remember about being brothers back then — the fighting, the forts, the farm, sharing a room?
- What was your relationship with Dad (Chuck) like, and did it feel different from his relationship with Shawn?
- What was your role in the family as the oldest? What did you feel responsible for?

## 2. The day of the accident
- What do you remember about that afternoon — getting off the bus, the gas run, the trip?
- Whose idea was it, and how did the plan come together in your memory?
- When did you find out something had gone wrong? What did you do?
- What has that day meant to you in the years since? Do you carry anything from it?

## 3. The surgery years
- What was it like watching your brother go through 40 surgeries?
- Where were you during all of it? What do you remember feeling?
- Is there anything from that time you'd handle differently now?

## 4. The distance between you now
- From your side, what happened between you and Shawn? How do you remember it?
- Is there anything you've wanted to say but haven't?
- What would it take, if anything, for things to be different?

## 5. You today
- What are you proudest of building — the farms, the life you made?
- What do you want your kids and grandkids to know about where you came from?
- If Shawn's readers only got to know you through this book, what's the one thing you'd want them to understand about you?
