# 🎙️ For Carina — In Your Own Words

Shawn's writing the book, but parts of it belong to you, and he can't write them — only you can.
This isn't an interview. Just hit record on your phone and talk. Ramble. Skip around. The
unpolished version is the one we want. There are no wrong answers, and nothing here has to
end up in the book unless you're okay with it.

The thread running through all of it: **at each stage, what did you see that he couldn't —
and what did you wish you could have said to him then?**

---

## 1. Before it all — who you were betting on

- When you married Shawn in Vegas, what did you think you were signing up for? What did you
  actually get?
- You became a lease agent and that money paid for Fielding. What was that like — going to
  work every day so he could chase a company? Did it ever feel unfair, or did it feel like
  *yours* too?
- What did you wish you could have said to him back then, in those earliest years?

## 2. Building Fielding — the garage years

- Take me into that period. The startup grind, the long hours, the uncertainty. What was it
  like to live next to it — to be married to someone who was always three steps ahead of
  where his feet were?
- What did you carry in those years that he never saw? What did you handle so he could build?
- Was there a moment you were scared it wouldn't work? A moment you knew it would?
- What did you wish you could have said to him during Fielding — and didn't?

## 3. The Quorum times — maybe the best of it

- This was probably the best stretch for the two of you. What made it good? What did the
  money, the stability, the arrival actually change between you?
- What do you remember about that period that he might have been too busy to notice?
- Is there something you wish you'd told him about how good it was — or a warning you wish
  you'd given about what came next?

## 4. The hard turn — and now

- Feb 2022 — you've called it the hardest, most painful time. Only say what you want to say.
  What was that like from inside it?
- The emotional wall — when he couldn't feel the big moments, even holding Gia. What was it
  like to love someone behind that wall? When did you first notice it?
- Therapy, "incrementally better" — what is actually better now, week to week, that you can
  point to?
- And the big one: **all the things you wished you could have said to him across these
  years — say them now. To him. On the record.**

---

*However much of this you want to do is the right amount. Even five minutes on one question
is a gift to the book — and to him.*
