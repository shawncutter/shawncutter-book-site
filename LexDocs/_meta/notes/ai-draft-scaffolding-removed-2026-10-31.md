# Social cut (≈180 words)

I numbered past versions of myself—#5 grunge and pissed, #9 invisible, #11 polished—because I kept molting to survive. Useful, but it blurred my face. The people closest to me often loved cached versions while I moved on. Partly my fault. I ship updates and forget release notes. They guess who walked in. Everyone pays. My fix is method over mask. Diagnose with doubt. Decide with responsibility. Execute with will. Recover with acceptance. Audit with first principles. Then communicate the update: what changed, what stayed, what I’m testing, how you’ll know it’s real. Epistemology with grease: Heat (friction without excuses), Weight (ten customers, real invoices, uptime), Repetition (same outcome in new light). If it holds, keep it. If it fails, learn.

Cutter Rule: If a belief can’t survive heat, weight, and repetition, it’s costume. Communication Rule: If your inner circle can’t describe the update, you haven’t shipped it. Field Test: Send one trusted person “Release Notes vNext”—four bullets. Ask for a read-back. Adjust toward reality. If this helps, share it with one builder who’s alone in the work.

# Publishing kit (fast)

Cadence: 2 posts/week for 8 weeks (15-post arc)

Length: 700–1,100 words primary; 150–200 words social cut

Structure: cold open scene → hinge (why it matters) → method (your rule) → turn (what you changed) → Cutter Rule → Field Test → Shawn Index Tags: identity, epistemology, vulnerability, communication, stoicism, entrepreneurship, resilience, EnergiAcres, Terrasow CTA: If this helps, share it with one builder who’s alone in the work. Shawn Index (0–10): Clarity, courage, communication. Score each; report composite.
