## Thesis (first person)

I am not chasing a church. I already walked that hallway. I studied religions and philosophies to see what people were trying to fill, and what they used the fill for. Where it landed: I am an atheist, the appreciative kind. Collective consciousness exists. We could be in a simulation right now. When it's over, it's over. That is actually what is beautiful about life — it happens, then it's gone. Make the most of it.

The chase of this book is not a company and it is not a campus. The chase is consciousness itself. We are all connected through a shared consciousness layer. Most of the time we cannot see it.

I felt that layer before I had language for it. Farm weather. Oilfield rooms. Founder chaos. Fatherhood. Those are portals as *feeling*, not brands. I will not mash them into a pitch. I will not turn them into a lecture about the universe.

What I will do is write the rooms where the layer was either blocked or briefly clear. If a page starts sounding like a manifesto, I have already lost the reader.
