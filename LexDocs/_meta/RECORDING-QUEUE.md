# RECORDING QUEUE — Say These Out Loud, I Write the Book

*Built 2026-08-29 from the manuscript audit. Eleven sessions. Each one produces a specific named piece. Work top to bottom — Tier 1 is the spine and it's the part that doesn't exist yet.*

---

## How this list is different from the other question files

You already have `running-questions.md`, `family-recording-questions.md`, `CONSOLIDATED-GAPS.md`, and four arc plans full of prompts. Those are **topic checklists** — they ask *what happened*.

The facts are already captured. `CHARACTER-LIST.md` has hundreds of them, and the Aug 2 call filled most of the remaining holes in the record. What's missing is not information. **It's the interior** — what you saw, what your body did, what you thought at the moment the thing turned.

So every question below is aimed at sensation, body, thought, and dialogue. Where I already have the fact, I say so, so you don't waste breath re-stating it.

---

## Recording rules that actually matter

1. **Answer in scene, not summary.** "It was a hard year" gives me nothing. "The gauze was dry and the nurse counted to three" gives me a paragraph.
2. **Say the specific noun.** 125cc Honda, not "the four-wheeler." Plymouth Voyager, not "the van." Mrs. Lang, not "my teacher." Every specific noun you say is a sentence I don't have to hedge.
3. **Don't skip the boring parts.** The twenty minutes fixing the hitch pin is the best thing in the accident story. Boring is where the real texture lives.
4. **Say "I don't remember" out loud.** Don't fill the gap. A recorded "I don't know, that part's just gone" is usable — it's honest and I can write around it or into it. An invented detail poisons the piece.
5. **Give me the body at the turn.** Hands, throat, stomach, temperature, what you were holding. Every piece that works — The Van, The Clothesline, 3:30 AM — works because the body is in it.
6. **Read the question, then be quiet.** Same rule you wrote for Mom's interview. Long pauses are where the material is.
7. **Don't self-edit.** Say the version you'd say to Andrew at 11pm, not the version you'd publish.

**Where to put the files:** `LexDocs/_inbox/YYYY-MM-DD-s01-october-29.md` (session number in the slug). Raw transcript, unedited — the `_inbox` README already covers routing. Tell me it's there and I'll write the piece.

**Session length:** 15–25 minutes each. Don't do more than two in a sitting. Sessions 1–4 will cost you something; space them out.
