# P2L Family Recordings — Ohio Edition
*Question sets for Kristen, Josh, Beth, and CJ — built to be handed out, recorded on a phone, and sent back.*

> *"The best stories aren't told by one person. They're told by everyone who was in the room."*

---

## 📋 HOW THIS WORKS (read this part to them)

1. **Open your phone's voice memo app.** That's it. No script, no prep.
2. **Read a question, then just talk.** 2–5 minutes each is plenty. Skip around if you want.
3. **Send the memos back to Shawn** — text, email, AirDrop, however's easiest.
4. Shawn's writing a book about the family. Your memories add dimension the rest of us can't — confirmation, correction, and the details only *you* remember.

**Ground rules:**
- There are no wrong answers.
- "I don't remember" is a useful answer — say it and move on.
- Say what *you* remember, not what you think happened.
- If it gets emotional, keep going or pause and come back. Either is fine.

## 👩 KRISTEN (Sister) — Kristin Anthony

*Three years younger than Shawn. The sister in a house built around brothers and a farm. She saw Chuck as a father to a daughter, watched the accident and surgeries from a younger vantage, and carries a version of this family the boys never had access to.*

### Your vantage
1. **Earliest farm memories.** What's the first thing you remember about the house, the land, a normal day growing up?
2. **Being the sister.** What was it like being the girl among CJ, Shawn — and later Josh? Where did you fit? What did they not let you into?
3. **Chuck as your dad.** Was he different with you than with the boys? What was he like with a daughter?
4. **Beth, from your side.** What did you see in your mom that the boys might have missed?

### The accident & after
5. **October 29, 1990.** You were around 8. Where were you when it happened? What do you remember about that day and the days after?
6. **How the house changed.** After the accident — the attention, the mood, the money, the worry. What shifted at home?
7. **The surgeries.** What was it like as a kid watching your brother get cut open again and again, year after year?

### Family dynamics
8. **The brothers fighting.** CJ and Shawn went at it. What did that look like from the outside? Did it scare you, annoy you, seem normal?
9. **Shawna.** What do you remember? How did you find out? What did it do to the family?
10. **Chuck's death — November 12, 2010.** Where were you? What do you remember about that day?

### For the book
11. **What do you wish people understood** about this family that they wouldn't guess from the outside?
12. **One thing to Shawn** — recorded, not face to face. What would you say?
13. **What are you proud of?** About him, about yourself, about this family.

## 👨 JOSH (Younger Brother) — Josh Cutter

*About ten years younger than Shawn — he was 19 when Shawn was 29. Josh grew up a decade behind the older boys, which means he knew a different Chuck, a different farm, and a different version of the household. He's the "different era" witness, and that's exactly what the book is missing.*

### Your era
1. **Your normal day.** Walk me through growing up — the house, the farm, the routines. What did a regular day look like for you?
2. **Chuck, to you.** We mostly have the older boys' version of Dad from the '80s. Yours is its own story. What was Chuck like *to you*?
3. **Did he change?** The older boys describe a certain Chuck from when they were young. Was the Chuck you knew in his 40s and 50s different — softer, harder, tired, mellowed? How?
4. **Beth, in your era.** What was your mom like during the years you remember best?

### The brothers
5. **Shawn, ten years up.** What was your relationship with him like growing up? Older hero, stranger, somewhere in between?
6. **CJ.** Same question — what was he to you?
7. **The accident, before your time.** You were tiny or not born when it happened. What did you grow up *knowing* about it? How was it talked about at home — openly, or never?

### Chuck's death (you'd have been ~21)
8. **November 12, 2010.** Where were you? What do you remember about that day and the days after?

### The land & the work
9. **Are you in it?** Are you involved in any of the Cutter / farm / EnergiAcres world now? (Shawn's seen the Cutter shirt.) What's your part?
10. **What does the land mean to you?** The Bechtel farm, the acreage — what's it worth to you, beyond money?

### For the book
11. **What only you know.** What's a memory or a fact about this family that nobody but you could give?
12. **One thing to Shawn** — recorded. What would you say?

## 👩 BETH (Mom) — Elizabeth "Beth" Cutter (née Bechtel)

*Beth is 67. The quiet center of the whole story. She saw everything — Chuck's intensity, the accident, the surgeries, the boys fighting, the farm holding together and falling apart. She's the one who kept the room standing.*

### The accident & hospital
1. **October 29, 1990.** Where were you when you found out? Who told you? What do you remember about that first hour?
2. **The hospital.** How long were you there? What did the doctors tell you? What did you *not* tell Shawn?
3. **The surgeries.** 40 of them over years. What was that like as a mother — driving your kid to be cut open again? Did it ever become routine, or was it always terrible?
4. **Did Shawn change?** Before the accident vs. after. What did you see shift — gradual or overnight?

### Chuck
5. **How did you meet Chuck?** What was he like when you were young?
6. **Chuck as a father.** What was he good at? What was he bad at? What do you wish he'd done differently?
7. **The hard parts.** You don't have to go deep — but what was the hardest thing about being married to Chuck Cutter?
8. **Shawna.** What was that like for you? How did you find out?
9. **Chuck's death — November 12, 2010.** Where were you? What happened? What do you remember about that day and the days after?

### The boys
10. **CJ and Shawn together.** What do you remember about them as kids — the fighting, but also the good? The forts, the animals, the fair.
11. **CJ and Shawn apart.** They don't talk now. What do you think happened? What do you wish for them?
12. **Who was Shawn as a kid,** before the accident? What did he care about? What made him laugh?

### The farm
13. **The Bechtel farm.** What does this land mean to you? How far back does it go? Why won't you sell it?
14. **Cedar Valley.** What was your experience of Cedar Valley Farms? What worked, what didn't, how did it feel when it fell apart?
15. **The yard.** Shawn says you're still out there at 67 — blowing off the yard, tending the garden, working the acreage. Why? What keeps you out there?

### For the book
16. **If you could say one thing to Shawn** you've never said — or said but he didn't hear — what would it be?
17. **What are you proud of?** About him, about yourself, about this family.

## 👨 CJ (Older Brother) — CJ Cutter

*The other half of "the Cutters outside." The shared bedroom, the forts, the fighting, the farm work. He's the one who called at 3:30 AM when Chuck died. The relationship is currently strained — these questions assume a willingness to participate that may not be there. If he says yes, it's gold.*

### Growing up
1. **The bedroom.** You shared a room with Shawn for years. What do you remember — the water beds, the TV, the fights?
2. **The forts.** You built them together. Whose idea was it? What was the best one?
3. **The fighting.** You were bigger, he was smaller. What do you remember? Did it feel fair? Did it ever go too far?
4. **Working together.** The farm, the animals, the fair. Did you resent the work or like it?

### The accident
5. **October 29, 1990.** Where were you? How did you find out? What do you remember about that day?
6. **After.** How did Shawn's accident change *your* life? What shifted for you?
7. **The wrestling tournament.** 3–4 months after the accident, Shawn came back. You got 2nd place that day. What do you remember?

### Chuck
8. **Your relationship with Dad.** What was it like — different from Shawn's?
9. **November 12, 2010.** You made the call at 3:30 AM. What happened that night?
10. **The funeral.** You spoke. What did you say? What do you remember about that day?

### Now
11. **What happened between you and Shawn,** from your side? What broke?
12. **One thing to him** — recorded, not face to face. What would you say?
