### FIELD
**Fielding Systems (2009-2015) — umbrella** — Shawn’s title at Fielding is **Founder / CEO** (his choice, 2026-10-31). Founded 2009 in the Plant City garage on two IKEA desks with Jeff Taynton (CTO) and Mike Huff; named with the name meant for his first son; built for Cutter Oil by translating CJ's requirements into software (the 60/40 split); oilfield software that came to monitor hundreds of thousands of wells across North America (incl. a 40,000-well West Texas EOR field); acquired by Quorum, closed March 23, 2015. Products (Shawn 2026-10-31, externally corroborated): FieldVisor (field data capture + production reporting), ScadaVisor (remote SCADA monitoring / field automation) and VisorBridge (their own cloud communication and polling engine, built later); FieldVisor Mobile and ScadaVisor Mobile also existed. Built as a platform play, one product a year (Shawn 2026-10-31): FieldVisor 2009, ScadaVisor 2010, FieldVisor Mobile 2011, VisorBridge 2012 — field data capture and production first, then SCADA and measurement, then communication/polling. There was NO polling engine before VisorBridge — it was the first, and it is a multi-threaded distributed application across several data-centre regions that holds socket connections open to field devices. FieldVisor Mobile was the mobile app for the hosted service. Customer mapping (Shawn 2026-10-31): Penn Virginia was an enterprise FieldVisor customer ONLY — it never ran VisorBridge, which was sold to Gulfport. Mobile history: the 2011 FieldVisor Mobile was built by a third-party firm and reached the App Store but ran badly (“didn’t run worth a crap”); Shawn pulled it and his team rebuilt it in-house in three weeks in 2012, against a 20 MB ceiling, then spent 90 days keeping it running.
