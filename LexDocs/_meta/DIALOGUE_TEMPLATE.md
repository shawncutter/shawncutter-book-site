## 🎬 HOW TO RUN THE DIALOGUE

1. **AI reads what's there.** No tool calls, no setup. Just reads and reflects back.
2. **AI asks the gap question that matters most** — not a list. One question. The right one.
3. **You answer** — voice memo, paste, or quick text.
4. **AI captures** what's new about your voice/people/world into `WRITING_PARTNER.md` and updates the running questions log.
5. **Loop until** we have what the GOAL specified.
6. **AI delivers** in dual-tier if requested:
   - **Free tier**: clean Medium-ready prose
   - **Paid tier**: writer's commentary, scenes-that-didn't-make-it, voice memo annotated, "what I almost wrote" alternatives

## 🔄 THE WORKFLOW (voice-first)

```
1. I record voice memo (5-15 min on iPhone, Otter, Whisper, etc.)
2. I transcribe (auto in Otter, manual paste, or Whisper CLI)
3. I open this template, fill TOPIC + GOAL + TIER + paste transcript
4. AI reads, reflects, asks the right question
5. I answer (more voice if needed)
6. AI delivers dual-tier output
7. AI updates WRITING_PARTNER.md and running-questions.md
8. I review, publish free tier to Medium, schedule paid tier for Substack/paywall
```
