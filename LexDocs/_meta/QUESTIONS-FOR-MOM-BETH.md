# Questions for Mom (Beth) — For the Book

*Mom — Shawn is writing the family story, and you're the one who actually remembers it right. A lot of this book only you can fill in. Just talk — record it on your phone if that's easiest, or write it out. Ramble, skip around, take your time. Long pauses are fine. Nothing here has to end up in the book unless you're okay with it. There are no wrong answers — we want the feeling and the detail, not a tidy report.*

---

## Part 1 — The accident & the surgery years (the biggest gap in the whole book)

*Everything about Shawn's accident and the ~40 surgeries is currently told from his side. You lived it as his mother. This is the single most important part.*

**The day it happened**
- Where were you and what were you doing when you found out?
- Who told you, and what exactly did they say?
- What's the first image or feeling you remember from that moment?
- What was the rush to him like — what went through your head on the way?
- When you first saw him — the injuries, the hospital — what do you remember?

**The first hours and days**
- Wooster Community Hospital, then the life-flight to Akron Children's — what was that stretch like for you?
- What were the doctors telling you? What were you most afraid of?
- Did you let yourself cry, or did you hold it together for everyone else? Why?
- Where were CJ, Kristin, and Josh? Who was holding the family together at home?

**The surgery years (~40 of them)**
- How did you get through that many surgeries? What did a "surgery day" look like for you?
- Which one was hardest on you — and why that one?
- Was there a moment you thought you might lose him? What happened?
- Did you protect Shawn from how scared you actually were?
- What did it cost you — physically, emotionally, financially — that nobody saw?

**Looking back**
- What are you proudest of in how you handled it?
- What do you wish you'd done differently?
- Is there something you've never told Shawn about that time that you'd want him to know now?
- If you could say one thing to the version of yourself standing in that hospital hallway — what would it be?

## Part 2 — The family backstory (you're the one who knows it)

*Shawn says he learned the real history from you, not from Dad. Help him get it right.*

**The Cutter grandparents**
- What were Grandma & Grandpa Cutter really like — their names, where they came from, who they were?
- The farm that was promised to Chuck and then sold out from under him — what actually happened, and when?
- They retired to Florida and the family lived there ~18 months around then. Why did that not work out?
- The story that Grandma wanted the grandkids to compete for her money — what did you see of that?

**Your side of the family — the Bechtels**
- The book's whole arc runs through *your* family's land, not Dad's. Tell the Bechtel story — the farm, how far it goes back, what it means to you.
- Buying great-grandma Bechtel's farm in 1990 — what do you remember about that move?
- Why won't you sell the main Bechtel farm? What would it mean to let it go?

**You and Chuck**
- How did you and Chuck actually meet?
- What was he like when you fell for him — before the intensity, the work, all of it?
- Living with his drive and his temper over the years — what was that really like?
- Shawna / the infidelity — only what you want to share. How did you find out, and how did you carry it?

**Uncle Gary**
- What do you remember about Gary — the Vietnam years, his struggles, the crises Chuck had to manage?
- How did Chuck carry being the one who always had to go get his older brother?
