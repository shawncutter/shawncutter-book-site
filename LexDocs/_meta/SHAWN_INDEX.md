# The Shawn Index — Voice-First Capture Scaffold

*The "Counting Shawns" piece references #5, #9, #11. The full count is undefined. This file is the scaffold for filling it in via voice memo. Record on the farm, on a walk, while driving — wherever the version-by-version archaeology comes naturally. Then we transcribe and integrate.*

---

## 🎙️ VOICE MEMO PROMPT (copy into your phone notes)

> "I'm going to walk through every version of myself I can remember, in roughly chronological order. For each one I'll try to capture: when, what triggered the molt, what costume I wore, what I was trying to survive, what it cost me, what I left behind when the next one started. I won't worry about being comprehensive. I'll just talk."

**Recommended length**: 15-25 minutes. You can break it into multiple sessions (one per Shawn, or a batch of three). Don't edit in your head — just talk.

---

## 📋 THE SCAFFOLD (fill via voice)

For each Shawn version, capture in order:

1. **Number / era marker** (e.g., "#3, age 12-13, after the second skin graft")
2. **The trigger** — what made the previous Shawn untenable?
3. **The costume** — what did I wear (literally and figuratively)? Music, clothes, friends, posture, how I introduced myself.
4. **What I was trying to survive** — the underlying pressure or fear
5. **What I told myself** — the internal narrative I ran
6. **The win** — what this version actually got me
7. **The cost** — what it took from me, who it pushed away
8. **The molt** — what made me shed it? Was it gradual or a single event?
9. **What I kept** when I moved on — the residue that carried into the next Shawn

## 🧠 STRUCTURAL QUESTIONS (the meta — answer once)

These are the "frame" questions that shape everything else.

### ✅ Q1 ANSWERED (Session 2026-05-09 dialogue)

**Where does the count start?**
> #0 = the pre-accident kid — fastest kid in school, future Joe Montana, Oct 28, 1990 version.
> #1 starts in the hospital bed.

**What this means structurally**: The index is a SURVIVAL LOG, not identity-craft. Every Shawn from #1 onward was born of necessity, not chosen. #0 got TAKEN. #1 had to be invented because the body the old kid lived in didn't work anymore. The molting wasn't pretentious philosophical experimentation — it was the survival mechanism that became habit.

**This reframes Counting Shawns**: The current piece treats the molting almost like vanity ("I went looking for something bigger than torque"). With #0/#1 as foundation, that line becomes survival, not curiosity. A 10-year-old whose body got ripped open looking for a reason to keep going.

### Remaining structural questions:
2. **Are the numbers continuous or sparse?** Did you actually count every version, or only the major ones? Are there #2 #3 #4 between #1 and #5, or do you jump?
3. **What's the unit of a "molt"?** A year? A relationship ending? A new job? A full identity overhaul? Or any of the above?
4. **Was the counting happening in real-time** (you knew you were #5 in HS) or is this retrospective archaeology?
5. **Is "Today's Shawn" #N or has the count ended?** Counting Shawns the piece suggests the method-over-mask shift is the END of counting. True? Or are we always counting?
6. **Who knows the count?** Has anyone ever heard about this before? Has Carina? Andrew? CJ? Or is this private accounting?
