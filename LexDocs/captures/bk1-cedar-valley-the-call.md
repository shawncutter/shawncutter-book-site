## Capture (verbatim, by session)

- **Session 5 — purpose:** "Get my mom to retirement. Build something sustainable, sell the business and the farm." Mom won't sell the main family farm but would part with the other farms. It didn't happen.
- **Session 5 — failure:** Broken business model. No sales. Didn't plan. Shawn was funding it personally. "A family business where everyone's emotions are involved — Shawn not funding it is somehow like turning against family." "You have to be here and in it and driving it or otherwise it doesn't work."
- **Session 5c — the break:** The team turned against him on calls, refusing directives. Not gradual. There was a moment the calls turned hostile.
- **Session 5f — the pattern:** Fiefdom / clique, "high school shit." Team on the call, Shawn remote. Time going into handwritten letters on every order. Shawn: "This is not going to help." Being direct was not enough.
- **Session 6 — the call:** "Stop selling to smokehouses. We had real retailers — grocery stores — and needed to only focus on that. What was working and what we could build. They refused. They wanted to put handwritten thank you notes in it. That's what they wanted to talk about. And I just like, okay, I give up."
- **Session 6 — now:** "Cedar Valley Farms is just about shut down. Moving on. Kept the wellness, but essentially the hemp operation is shut down. The farm remained. Cedar Valley Farms was really the hemp and wellness — Cutter Farm still operates. Had to shut down CVF and take the loss, take the L, and move on. Lesson learned, especially in government regulation and bullshit."
- **Beth thread (Session 4/5):** The farm is Mom's, Bechtel land, same farm since Ohio was a state. At 67 she is still working it.
