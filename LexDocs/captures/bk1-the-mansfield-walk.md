## Beat: What keeps me up

Getting Mansfield to the finish line. Understanding the fundraise that needs to happen in order to build the team to do what I need to do.

The market has shifted. The aim of EnergiAcres needs to have an impact binding to all of the existing projects that are already started. I did not anticipate how fast the market would move, how fast the gas would be claimed, how fast the power would be claimed, how fast all of it could move.

It's never moved this fast. And so that all keeps me up.
