**Thesis (PHILOSOPHY-BRIEF, first person, his words):**

- "Ever since my accident, I developed a way of thinking — of walking through the past and history, cause and effect. But eventually we see there are turtles all the way down."
- "I don't think I have stopped digging. But I have changed it. I seek the vastness of human experience — the highs and the lows — and I want to experience it with the people I care about."
- "Philosophy, for me, was understanding the mind: how did we come to know what we know? How do we understand why and how things happened? Bridging the collective consciousness."
- "The chase of this book is consciousness itself — that is locked. This is the thinking underneath the chase: if no single mind can reach the bottom, the work is lateral. Connect minds. What one can't hold alone, many can hold together."
