## Why it matters to the book

The Foundry arc is the thinnest in the manuscript — nine scenes — and its
subject is legacy and land. This is the first new land event in the corpus since
Cedar Valley Farms in 2020, and the first one Shawn has entered into with Josh
rather than CJ. `bk1-the-day-i-stopped-recruiting-my-family.md` and
`bk1-the-quiet-quit-of-legacy.md` are both about family and business going wrong;
whatever this is, it is a later data point on the same thread.

It also lands 36 years after the purchase in
`bk1-farm-roots-shawn-dictation.md` — "when i was 11, we purchased my great
grandparents farm." The rhyme is noted, not written: whether it means anything is
his to say, not the file's.
