## Beat: Why I wouldn't lead it

And so I thought that meant I couldn't really lead it from the beginning.

I see now that I probably could've played a different role, one that kept me as CEO but raised me above. That was really my mistake. I didn't know how to do that.

And I thought, if I was in the CEO role and it was too far along, I would be sucked in. And I just could not allow myself to get acquired again.

And so much of what I've dealt with, I've become a necessary component. So I was very much trying to keep myself from being in that place. I thought that meant starting with a few cofounders that could take it forward.

I did not want to be acquired. Meaning, if I help bring technology into startups, the IP has to stay with the company and NOT Shawn Cutter. I cannot be part of a deal happening or not.

I had reached a point where I had removed certain chains of life and wanted to grow my fortune while focusing on startups that can have an impact, and just fun startups. I wanted to make my work fun and part of life.
