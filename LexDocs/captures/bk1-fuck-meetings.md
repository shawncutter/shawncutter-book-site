**Working title:** Fuck Meetings

After a few months at Quorum, I started noticing a pattern in the Houston meetings. Eight, ten people in a room. Half of them didn't say a word. I didn't get the purpose.

A year in, I'd had it.

I wanted to map salary bands — associate, director, VP. Rough benchmarks at each level so we could calculate what these meetings were costing. By my estimates, some of them were running three thousand, four thousand dollars an hour. We just couldn't operate that way if no value was being created.

I became known for walking out. If a meeting had no clear purpose, I'd stand up and leave. Hence the fuck meetings.

I wanted to integrate a ticker into Outlook. The cost of the meeting would always be going, and everyone would be made aware. Get in, get the information, get out.

I asked for the average salary levels for each level. I got shut down on the project.

The ask that would have made the cost undeniable stopped there. Without the numbers, the cost stayed an estimate in my head. The rooms kept meeting.

If the room isn't creating value, leave — and make the cost visible.
