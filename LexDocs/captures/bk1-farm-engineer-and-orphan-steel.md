> "I first got the idea about Orphan Well Steel because I was already looking at the orphan well programs and just it dawned on me that we're never gonna be able to plug all the wells, that we don't even have enough service rigs to plug all of the wells. And it was during that time I was also putting together and learning about LEED certification and efficiencies and just the building requirements and how to build better.
