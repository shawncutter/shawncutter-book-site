# EXTRACTION — what's new

## ⭐ The line the book was missing

> **"I think it held you back from knowing what you would have done."**

Not *it held you back*. It held you back from **knowing**. That is the thesis of the Fractured arc
in eleven words, and it came from Beth unprompted. Candidate title, candidate epigraph, candidate
last line of the arc.

## New facts — the surgery years, from her side

- **Dr. Wagner** — ⭐ NEW CHARACTER. The surgeon. Beth wants to sit down with him: *"hear his side.
  What he thought at the time, did he really have faith that it was going to…"* — she never learned
  whether he believed it would work. **New interview target; see `interview-wagner-surgeon.md` gap.**
- **The scariest surgery, from her side:** the lat dorsi flap. *"I could not even imagine, any way,
  that you would have your foot."*
- **The reveal after the flap surgery — the foot didn't look like a foot.** Enormous. *"How is this
  going to look like a foot again?"* Staff kept saying *"it will go down."*
- ⭐ **"Your dad almost passed out, which I said that, and I didn't."** Chuck — the man who tied a
  steer on a lawn — nearly went down at the sight of it. Beth stayed standing. **The single best
  character beat in the whole transcript.** It inverts the entire Chuck/Beth dynamic in one clause.
- **Skin:** nothing on the body replaces the skin on the sole of a foot. Beth: *"I've seen it for
  myself."* Multiple donor sites attempted. Shawn asked about taking it from his right foot — refused.
- **They tried to grow skin,** sourced from further off, several separate attempts.
- ⭐ **The Buehler's prescription.** A ~$500–$1,000 skin-growth prescription, filled at a grocery
  pharmacy in ~1991. The counter staff couldn't believe the price. Beth: *"I'm going to write a
  check for it."* — A farm mother writing a four-figure check at a grocery store counter. That's a
  scene, whole, as-is.
- **The tech closed the loop:** that compound is now a spray used on burn victims — and Shawn uses
  the same growth factor on tattoos today.

  could have, but nobody said that to us."*

## New facts — recovery and acceptance

- ⭐ **"I didn't accept it until sophomore or junior year."** ~1995–96. Five or six years. That's the
  date-stamp the Fractured arc has never had.

  but I was walking on it."* He couldn't feel it, so he never offloaded it. **And the physical
  therapists never told him.** He learned the mechanics of his own foot thirty years late.
